Information We Collect

Velvet Trails collects various types of information to operate our Site, provide our content, and display relevant advertising. We collect both automatically generated information and, in some cases, information you voluntarily provide.

  • Automatically Collected Information: When you visit sixstarsdestinations.com, our servers and third-party services automatically record certain information that your browser sends. This data may include:

    • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address.
    • Browser type and version.
    • Device type and operating system.
    • Pages you visit on Velvet Trails.
    • The time and date of your visit.
    • The time spent on those pages.
    • Referring website addresses.
    • Demographic data and interests (inferred through analytics and advertising services).
    • Information about your interactions with advertisements displayed on the Site.

    This information is primarily non-personally identifiable and is used for analytical purposes, to improve the user experience, and to deliver targeted advertising.

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: While Velvet Trails does not sell products directly, you may choose to provide us with personal information in other ways, such as:

    • When you submit comments on our blog posts.
    • When you subscribe to a newsletter (if offered).
    • When you contact us directly via email or a contact form.

    This information may include your name, email address, and any other details you choose to share. We only collect this information if you voluntarily submit it to us.

How We Use Information

Velvet Trails uses the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Operate and Maintain Velvet Trails: To ensure the proper functioning and security of sixstarsdestinations.com.
  • To Improve User Experience: We use aggregated information to understand how our Users as a group use the services and resources provided on our Site. This helps us to personalize content and improve the overall design and functionality of Velvet Trails.

  • To Understand Audience Demographics and Interests: We analyze data to gain insights into our audience's demographics and interests, which helps us tailor our editorial content on luxury hidden gems and travel to better suit our readers.
  • To Respond to Inquiries: If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your questions, comments, or requests.
  • To Comply with Legal Obligations: We may use your information as necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or governmental requests.

Please note that Velvet Trails does not sell products directly, and therefore, your information is not used for direct product sales transactions on our Site.

Cookies and Advertising

Velvet Trails (sixstarsdestinations.com) uses "cookies" to enhance your experience. A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a web server. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

  • How We Use Cookies: We use cookies for two primary purposes:

    • Analytics: To track and analyze user activity on Velvet Trails, helping us understand how visitors interact with our content and improve our Site.
    • Advertising: To display personalized advertisements through Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These cookies help to make advertising more relevant to you by collecting information about your browsing habits and interests.

  • Google AdSense / Google Publisher Tag: Velvet Trails utilizes Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google uses cookies, including DART cookies, to serve ads based on your visit to sixstarsdestinations.com and other sites on the Internet. The Google Publisher Tag Network ID for Velvet Trails is `/21679776076/sixstarsdestinations.com`. This allows Google and its partners to serve ads to our Users based on their visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • Managing Cookies: You have the option to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of Velvet Trails and other websites you visit. You can also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page or the Network Advertising Initiative's opt-out page.

Third-Party Services (Google)

Velvet Trails relies on third-party services, primarily Google, to provide analytics and advertising services. These third parties may collect information directly from you or from your device when you visit sixstarsdestinations.com.

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect information about how visitors use our Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This information is used to compile reports and help us improve the Site. Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics about your visits to Velvet Trails is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Please be aware that Velvet Trails does not control the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of Google and any other third-party services linked from our Site to understand their data collection and usage practices.
